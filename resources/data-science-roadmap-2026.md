## Preview of the path

1. **Python foundations** — syntax, functions, data structures  
2. **Tables & analysis** — pandas, cleaning, EDA  
3. **SQL** — extract and aggregate real data  
4. **Statistics for decisions** — enough to reason about results  
5. **Machine learning basics** — baselines before complexity  
6. **Portfolio** — 2–3 documented projects
