Hi, I'm the person behind Techtonz.

I started this site because most of what I found online about AI, data science, and programming fell into two camps: dense academic writing that assumes you already know the material, or shallow listicles that don't actually teach you anything. I wanted something in between — writing that respects your time and your intelligence, without gatekeeping behind jargon.

## What I write about

Techtonz covers the things I spend most of my own time learning, building with, and helping others navigate:

- **Artificial intelligence** — practical tools, not just headlines
- **Data analytics** — turning raw data into decisions that hold up
- **Data science** — real analysis, real datasets, real code
- **Python programming** — from first principles to production
- **Machine learning** — models, training, and workflows that actually ship
- **Career guides** — breaking into tech and growing once you're in
- **Scholarship opportunities** — funded routes into tech education, curated and kept current

## Why "Techtonz"

Technology is constantly shifting — new tools, new frameworks, new ways of working. The name is a nod to that movement: tectonic shifts, but for tech.

## What to expect

New articles are published regularly, and the goal is always the same: give you something you can actually use, not just something to skim. If there's a topic you want covered, [reach out](/contact/) — I read everything.
