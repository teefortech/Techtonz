# 5 Fully Funded Scholarships for Aspiring Data Scientists in 2026

Funding remains one of the biggest barriers to pursuing higher education in [Data Science](/data-science/) and [Artificial Intelligence](/artificial-intelligence/). Fortunately, governments, universities, and international organizations continue to invest in talented students by offering fully funded scholarships that can significantly reduce—or even eliminate—the cost of earning a postgraduate degree.

Whether you're a recent graduate, a data analyst looking to upskill, or a professional planning a career transition into AI and data science, these scholarships are worth exploring.

Many fully funded scholarships provide:

- Full tuition fees
- Monthly living allowance
- Accommodation support
- Health insurance
- Return airfare
- Visa or settlement allowances (for eligible programmes)

> **Note:** Scholarship requirements, deadlines, and benefits change regularly. Always verify the latest information on the official scholarship website before applying.

---

## How to Identify a Legitimate Scholarship

Before investing time in any application, ensure the scholarship is genuine.

Here are a few things to check:

- It is sponsored by a recognized government, university, or reputable organization.
- The eligibility criteria are publicly available.
- There is no application fee.
- Official contact information uses a verified institutional email address.
- Previous scholarship recipients or programme details are publicly documented.

If a scholarship requests payment before processing your application, treat it as a major red flag.

## 1. Chevening Scholarships (United Kingdom)

Official website:
https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/

Chevening Scholarships are funded by the UK Government through the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO). They support outstanding professionals from more than 160 countries to pursue a one-year Master's degree at a UK university.

Many recipients study programmes such as:

- Data Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Statistics
- Computer Science
- Business Analytics

### What it covers

- Full tuition fees
- Monthly stipend
- Return flights
- Visa application costs
- Arrival and departure allowances

### Best suited for

Professionals with leadership potential and relevant work experience who want to study in the UK.

---

## 2. Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Scholarships (Europe)

Official website:
https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/opportunities-for-individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters

Erasmus Mundus offers one of the most prestigious international scholarship programmes in Europe. Students study at two or more universities in different European countries and graduate with a joint or multiple Master's degree.

Many programmes are available in:

- Data Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Big Data
- Machine Learning
- Computational Statistics

### What it covers

- Full tuition
- Monthly living allowance
- Travel expenses
- Installation allowance
- Insurance

### Best suited for

Students seeking international academic exposure while studying across multiple European universities.

---

## 3. DAAD Scholarships (Germany)

Official website:
https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/

The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) supports thousands of international students every year.

Although DAAD funds many disciplines, there are numerous opportunities for students pursuing postgraduate degrees in:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Science
- Computer Science
- Data Analytics
- Applied Mathematics

### What it may cover

- Monthly stipend
- Health insurance
- Travel allowance
- Research support
- Tuition support (where applicable)

Germany continues to be one of the most attractive destinations for students seeking high-quality education with relatively low tuition costs.

---

## 4. Fulbright Foreign Student Program (United States)

Official website:
https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/

The Fulbright Foreign Student Program enables talented students from around the world to pursue Master's and PhD degrees in the United States.

Although it is not exclusively designed for data science students, many award recipients study:

- Data Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Statistics
- Computer Science
- Information Systems

### Funding usually includes

- Tuition fees
- Living allowance
- Health insurance
- Airfare
- Research support (where applicable)

Fulbright is highly competitive and places significant emphasis on academic excellence and leadership potential.

---

## 5. Commonwealth Scholarships (United Kingdom)

Official website:
https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/

The Commonwealth Scholarship Commission funds postgraduate study for students from eligible Commonwealth countries, including Nigeria.

Many scholars pursue degrees in:

- Data Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Computer Science
- Applied Statistics
- Information Technology

### What it usually covers

- Full tuition fees
- Monthly living allowance
- Return flights
- Thesis grants (where applicable)
- Additional study-related allowances

The programme aims to equip graduates with the knowledge and skills needed to contribute to the development of their home countries.

# How to Improve Your Chances of Winning a Scholarship

Competition for fully funded scholarships is intense, but careful preparation can make your application stand out.

Here are some practical tips:

- Maintain strong academic performance.
- Build a portfolio of Python and data science projects.
- Gain relevant work or volunteer experience.
- Write a compelling Statement of Purpose (SOP).
- Secure strong recommendation letters.
- Apply for multiple scholarships instead of relying on just one.
- Begin preparing your documents several months before application deadlines.
